This audio begins with a traditional Guru invocation, offering salutations to the Guru as Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwara, and as Dakshinamurti—the source of all knowledge and the healer of worldly afflictions. With reverence to the Guru’s lotus feet, the discourse then transitions into the third Panchadi of the text.

In the earlier Panchadis, the waters were praised and their glories acknowledged. In this third Panchadi, the focus shifts from praise to prayer—specifically, a prayer requesting the waters (Jalābhimāni Devatās) to remove obstacles and unfavorable conditions that could hinder the continuation of a Yajña.

Each mantra is explained carefully through anvaya. The waters are invoked to drive away destructive external forces, such as fire that could damage the Yajñaśālā or surroundings, as well as internal disturbances, like bodily heat that manifests as fever. The prayer also seeks removal of negative forces—Rakṣasas, diseases, sins, and their root causes. Further, it asks for the elimination of deficiencies in the senses and for relief from poverty or lack of resources, ensuring both physical well-being and material sufficiency for sacred duties.

Rather than listing obstacles one by one, the later mantra comprehensively requests the removal of all inauspicious influences from the place of worship. The section concludes with a Śānti Mantra, invoking completeness, peace, and auspicious closure before moving on to the next Panchadi in the following recording.
