This recording covers the 15th panchadi, which opens the fifth anuvakam. The content continues the description of Shishira Ritu (the winter season), focusing specifically on the Vishwadevas — the presiding deities of this season.

The first set of mantras paints a vivid picture of the Vishwadevas' appearance and power. They are described as wearing deeply red-colored garments — "atitamrani vasagumsi" — signifying their intense, fiery nature. They carry formidable weapons: Ashti (a weapon fashioned from bones), Vajri (a thunderbolt-like club similar to Indra's Vajrayudham), and Shatghni (a weapon capable of slaying hundreds simultaneously). Armed with these, the Vishwadevas "vipraharanti" — they strike down, drive away, and overpower all enemies and hostile forces. Their tongues are said to be fire itself (Agnijihva), which carries a dual meaning: literally, Agni resides in their tongues, and symbolically, the oblations (ahuthis) offered into the sacred fire are received and accepted by the Vishwadevas. Following their victory, they make triumphant proclamations — referred to as "asaschata," or victorious speech.

The next set of mantras contains the actual words of that victorious proclamation. The Vishwadevas declare that no being — whether god, mortal, the mighty king Varuna, Agni, Indra, or Vayu — is equal to them in power. This bold assertion is not mere boasting; it is presented as the natural expression of supremacy after vanquishing all opposing forces. The listing of major deities like Indra, Varuna, Agni, and Vayu makes the declaration all the more emphatic, underscoring that the Vishwadevas stand unrivaled.

The final two mantras describe the extraordinary scale of the bow wielded by the Vishwadevas. One end of the bow rests in the heavens (div) and the other end is anchored on the earth (prithvi). This imagery serves as a powerful metaphor for their cosmic magnitude and unmatched might — beings whose weapons span the entire universe are truly without equal.
The panchadi as a whole glorifies the Vishwadevas as the supreme guardians of the Shishira season, mighty in form, fearsome in battle, and unparalleled in the cosmos.
