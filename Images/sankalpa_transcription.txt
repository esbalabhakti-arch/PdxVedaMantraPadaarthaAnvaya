So let us look at a simple saṅkalpam.
​
So we first do ācamanaṁ and follow that with:

शुक्लांबरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम्।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये॥
​

and do prāṇāyāmam.
​

And then following that we say the following saṅkalpam, and it goes as follows:

ममोपात्त समस्त दुष्कृतक्षयद्वारा श्रीपरमेश्वर प्रीत्यर्थं
or
श्रीमन्नारायण प्रीत्यर्थं शुभे शोभने मुहूर्ते अद्य ब्रह्मणः द्वितीय पारार्धे श्वेतवाराह कल्पे वैवस्वत मन्वन्तरे अष्टाविंशतितमे कलियुಗೆ प्रथमे पादे जम्बूद्वीपे भारतवर्षे भरतखण्डे मेरोः दक्षिणे पार्श्वे शाकाब्दे अस्मिन् वर्तमान व्यावहारिके प्रभवाति षष्टि संवत्सराणां मध्ये विश्वावसु नाम संवत्सरे उत्तरायणे…
​

Now I’m here giving an example of the yamatarpaṇam that came in, I think, June or July this year.
​

Okay, so:

उत्तरायणे ग्रीष्मऋतौ मिथुनमासे कृष्णपक्षे चतुर्दश्यां शुभतिथौ भौमवासरयुक्तायां रोहिणीनक्षत्रयुक्तायां शूलयोगे भद्रकरणकेयुक्तायाम् एवं गुणविशेषणविशिष्टायां अस्यां चतुर्दश्यां यमधर्मराज प्रीत्यर्थं कृष्णाङ्गारक चतुर्दशी पुण्यकाले यमतर्पणं करिष्ये॥
​

Okay, so here you will notice that we initially start at the cosmic level, or at the macro level, which means we started from the time of creation.
​
So we say adya brahmaṇaḥ, which means the current Brahmā.
​
अद्य ब्रह्मणः द्वितीय पारार्धे, like I explained earlier, dvitīya means second and parārdhe is the other half, so dvitīya parārdhe is the second half, which means his first 50 years are over and we are on the 51st year.
​

And the kalpam is that 1000-year—1000 catur-yugams together constitutes one kalpam, one daytime, and that is named as śvetavarāha.
​
So we are in श्वेतवाराह कल्पम् वैवस्वत मन्वन्तरे, which means we are in the manvantara by Vaivasvata (Vivasvān).
​
So वैवस्वत मन्वन्तरे, and in that we are in the 28th catur-yugam.
​

So अष्टाविंशति—aṣṭāviṁśati means 28, अष्टाविंशतितमे कलियुगे.
​
So in that catur-yugam the first three yugams have already passed by.
​
So now we are in kaliyugam, कलियुगे, and प्रथमे पादे.
​
There it means the kaliyugam is split into four parts and we are in the first part.
​
So each part is 108,000 years.
​

So now we have come to the human years at this point: प्रथमे पादे जम्बूद्वीपे.
​
So we are talking as if we are doing saṅkalpam in India, and when you are doing the saṅkalpam in America you would say क्रौञ्चद्वीपे रमणकवर्षे इन्द्रखण्डे, and we will see later what these three mean, and how many are there, and what different people in different continents say, and things like that.
​

So, and then: मेरोः दक्षिणे पार्श्वे.
​
Now this is the direction in which the land mass of India is sitting, which is to the south of the Meru mountain, so मेरोः दक्षिणे पार्श्वे.
​

अस्मिन् वर्तमान व्यावहारिके.
​
Now here we say vyavahārike because whatever follows from here that we are describing is not at the cosmic level, but it is at a transactional level.
​
And why is it at the transactional level?
Because we are talking of basically a year followed by the season, followed by the month, followed by, you know, the pakṣam and the tithi, and so on, right.
​

So this is more a calendar that has been set up for daily transactional purposes, and this by itself is at a very micro level compared to the cosmic level.
​
So one reason why—that is the reason why we say व्यावहारिके प्राभवाधिकषष्टि संवत्सराणां मध्ये विश्वावसु नाम संवत्सरे.
​
Prabhavādi means Prabhava is the first year; if you were to list all the 60 years, and our calendar has 60 names for each of the years, and then that repeats again.
​
So that is why प्राभवाधिकषष्टि संवत्सराणां मध्ये, in those 60 years, विश्वावसु नाम संवत्सरे, which means we are in the year called Viśvavasu.
​

Then once you talk of the year, the highest level is the division as uttarāyaṇam and dakṣiṇāyaṇam.
​
So we say उत्तरायणे, then ṛtu, because again ṛtu is at a higher level compared to month.
​
So I’ll explain later how many ṛtus are there, or how many months are there, and what is the difference between chandramānam and sauramānam.
​

So we say उत्तरायणे, and then the higher level is ṛtu.
​
So we say the ṛtu name, which is in this case ग्रीष्मऋतौ, ग्रीष्मऋतौ मिथुनमासे, then māsam is divided again into two parts: śukla pakṣam and kṛṣṇa pakṣam.
​

So for yamatarpaṇam, the definition itself is: it has to be kṛṣṇa pakṣam caturdaśī, plus another most important factor that has to coincide is it has to be on a Tuesday, because it has to be aṅgāraka caturdaśī.
​
So it has to be a Tuesday, and kṛṣṇa pakṣam, and caturdaśī; when these three coincide that becomes the puṇya kālam for yamatarpaṇam.
​

So in this saṅkalpam it would come as:

कृष्णपक्षे चतुर्दश्यां शुभतिथौ भौमवासरयुक्तायां रोहिणीनक्षत्रयुक्तायां शूलयोगे भद्रकरणकेयुक्तायाम् एवं गुणविशेषणविशिष्टायाम् अस्यां चतुर्दश्यां यमधर्मराज प्रीत्यर्थं कृष्णाङ्गारक चतुर्दशी पुण्यकाले यमतर्पणं करिष्ये॥
​

Like I said, it has to be a Tuesday, bhauma means Tuesday (bhūmiputra), bhūmavasara in this case.
​
It was Rohiṇī nakṣatram yuktāyām, and Śūla yoga.
​

Ganda yoga is the yogam that follows Śūla yogam, but at a particular time it changes from Śūla yogam to Ganda yogam.
​
So depending on which time you are performing, you have to choose the appropriate yoga and say that.
​
And bhadrakaraṇa, again there will be a time at which bhadrakaraṇam ends, so if you are doing it past that time then you will say śakunīkaraṇa yuktāyām.
​

Then evam guṇa, means with extra, additional specialities and qualities, asyām viśeṣaṇa-viśiṣṭāyām asyām caturdaśyām.
​
Then what is the purpose of doing?
Yama dharma rāja prītyartham kṛṣṇa aṅgāraka caturdaśī puṇya kāle yamatarpaṇam kariṣye.
​

So this is one simple way of starting a saṅkalpam, that is you start with:

शुभे शोभने मुहूर्ते अद्य ब्रह्मणः द्वितीय पारार्धे… and so on.
​

So now here you will notice that, in the time that we were talking about, that is the time at which you are doing, compared to when you specify it with respect to starting from creation, what we said was, especially when we come to the vyavahārika details, we said ग्रीष्मऋतौ and उत्तरायणे.
​

Now uttarāyaṇam and dakṣiṇāyaṇam is pretty straightforward to understand, and we know that when the Sun starts its movement from the southern hemisphere to the northern hemisphere it is uttarāyaṇam, and when it comes from north to south it is dakṣiṇāyaṇam—we know that.

So the other thing was the ṛtu.
